Full SaaS idea-to-verdict pipeline. Takes an idea from raw concept through build, launch, and measurement with kill gates at every stage.

Usage: /saas-cycle [1-3 sentence idea]

Examples:
- /saas-cycle AI tool that generates SOC 2 compliance docs from your codebase
- /saas-cycle Shopify app that A/B tests product descriptions using AI
- /saas-cycle SaaS that monitors competitor pricing and alerts on changes

This is the master pipeline. It runs all 6 phases with kill gates:

SHAPE (2 days — kill bad ideas fast):
  1. Scout — Pain validation, ICP, distribution hypothesis
  2. Vex — Technical feasibility
  3. Sage — Compliance pre-check

VALIDATE (1 week — prove the market):
  4. Atlas — Market sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM)
  5. Arya — Architecture + sprint plan [Yash approval gate]
  6. Riko — Project scaffold
  7. Ledger — Pricing + unit economics

BUILD (2-4 weeks — ship the MVP):
  8. Rex orchestrates: Nova → Koda (phased) → Luna → Quill → Vega

LAUNCH (1 week — go to market):
  9. Echo — Distribution plan + launch sequence
  10. Mira — Knowledge capture
  11. Bolt — Deploy + launch ops
  12. Hawk — Monitoring setup

MEASURE (30-90 days — learn from reality):
  13. Orbit — Metrics framework
  14. Pulse — User research

DECIDE (1 day — binary answer):
  15. Verdict — SCALE / PIVOT / KILL

Kill gates checked after each phase. Pipeline halts on KILL verdict.
Rex coordinates all handoffs and verifies every agent's output.

Expected outcome: ~1 in 5 ideas makes it to SCALE. The other 4 get killed early, saving weeks of wasted effort. That's the system working correctly.
