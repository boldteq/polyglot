## PDP Toggle Labels

### One-time option label
| Copy | Chars | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| "One-time purchase" | 18 | Default — clear, no commitment |
| "Buy once" | 8 | Casual/direct |
| "One-time" | 8 | Minimal |

### Subscribe option label
| Copy | Chars | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| "Subscribe & save 20%" | 21 | Default — shows savings % |
| "Subscribe & save $X/order" | 26 | Dollar amount (more tangible) |
| "Auto-ship — save 20%" | 21 | Replenishment framing |
| "Subscribe: $X/month" | 20 | Price-forward |
| "Join the routine" | 17 | Brand-voice (lifestyle brands) |

**Rule:** Always show savings % or $ amount next to subscribe option. Never "Subscribe" alone — must show the value.

## Frequency Selector Labels

| Option | Label | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Every 2 weeks | "Every 2 weeks" | High-frequency replenishment (pet food, protein) |
| Every month | "Every month" (default) | Most brands' sweet spot |
| Every 6 weeks | "Every 6 weeks" | Moderate usage products |
| Every 2 months | "Every 2 months" | Low-frequency supplements, skincare |
| Every 3 months | "Every 3 months" | 90-day supply products |
| Every 6 months | "Every 6 months" | Max reasonable cadence |

**Rule:** 3-5 options maximum. More → analysis paralysis.

## Savings Display Formats

Show BOTH % AND $ amount when possible.

| Format | Copy | When to use |
|--------|------|-------------|
| % + $ | "Save 20% / $5.80 per order" | Default — most clear |
| % only | "Save 20%" | Space-constrained slots |
| $ only | "Save $5.80 each delivery" | Low-price items where % feels small |
| Annual framing | "Save $69.60/year" | High-consideration products |
| Per-unit | "Just $1.09/serving" | Supplements, food, beverages |

## Subscription Portal Labels

### Account management actions
All must be EQUAL visual prominence. No hiding cancel behind other actions.

| Action | Button label | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| Skip next delivery | "Skip next order" | No fee, lowest friction |
| Pause subscription | "Pause subscription" | Pause for 1-3 months |
| Swap product | "Swap product" | Change to different SKU |
| Change frequency | "Change frequency" | Address "too much" issue |
| Cancel | "Cancel subscription" | MUST be same size/prominence as others |

**Rule:** Cancel button must be same size, same color treatment, same visual weight as other management options. Making it smaller or gray = dark pattern.

## Cancel Save Flow Copy

### Step 1: Reason + Pause Offer

**Cancellation reason prompt:**
```
Why are you cancelling?
○ It's too expensive
○ I have too much product
○ I'm not seeing results
○ I want to try something else
○ I'm going through a difficult time
○ Other reason
```

**Response by reason:**

| Reason selected | Save offer |
|----------------|------------|
| Too expensive | "How about pausing for a month? We'll hold your spot, no charges." |
| Too much product | "Skip your next 2 deliveries — resume when you're ready." |
| Not seeing results | "Give it one more month — most customers see results by week 6." |
| Try something else | "Swap to [product B] and keep your subscriber discount." |
| Difficult time | "Pause for up to 3 months, no questions asked." |

**CTA options:**
- "Pause for 1 month" | "Skip next 2 orders" | "Yes, swap product" | "Continue to cancel"

**Rule:** Offer must address the stated reason. Generic save offers fail.

---

### Step 2: Discount Offer

```
Headline: "One more thing before you go"
Sub: "What if we made it more affordable?"
Offer: "Get 25% off your next 3 orders"
CTA: [Claim 25% off]   [No thanks, continue]
```

**Discount amount:**
- Low margin products: 10-15%
- Medium margin: 20-25%
- High margin: 30-40%
- Rule: Never offer more than your typical new-customer discount

---

### Step 3: Final Confirmation

```
Headline: "Confirm cancellation"
Body: "Your subscription will end on [date]. You won't be charged after [date].
      You can resubscribe anytime."
CTA: [Cancel my subscription]   [Keep my subscription]
```

**Rules:**
- Final step must have a clear path to cancel (no more offers)
- Buttons equal size/prominence
- State the final date and financial consequence clearly
- No guilt-trip copy ("Cancel and miss out on X benefits")

## Pre-Renewal Reminder Email

| Slot | Copy | Chars |
|------|------|-------|
| Subject line | "Your [Product] ships in 5 days" | 31 |
| | "[Product] order coming up — manage your subscription" | 51 |
| | "Quick heads up: Your order ships [date]" | 39 |
| Preview text | "Skip, pause, or swap before [date]" | 34 |
| Headline | "Your next delivery is almost here" | 33 |
| Body intro | "Hi [First name], your [Product] subscription renews on [date] for $[amount]." | variable |
| Management CTA | "Manage subscription" | 21 |
| Skip CTA | "Skip this order" | 15 |

**Timing:** Send 5-7 days before billing date. Earlier = forgotten. Day-of = no time to act.

## Trial-to-Paid Conversion Copy

### Trial CTA on landing page
| Copy | Chars | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| "Start your trial for $X" | 22 | Low-commitment entry |
| "Try it for $X" | 14 | Minimal |
| "Begin your trial" | 17 | Action-forward |
| "Get started — $X trial" | 22 | Price transparent |

**Never:** "Subscribe" as trial CTA — triggers commitment aversion.

### Trial welcome email subject
| Copy | Chars |
|------|-------|
| "Your trial is active — here's what to expect" | 46 |
| "Welcome to your [X]-day trial" | 30 |
| "Your [Product] trial starts today" | 33 |

### Pre-end trial email (5 days before)
| Subject | Chars |
|---------|-------|
| "Your trial ends in 5 days — continue your results?" | 51 |
| "Don't lose your progress — trial ends [date]" | 45 |
| "5 days left to keep [Benefit]" | 30 |

### Anti-dark-pattern rule: Trial cancellation
- No save flow on trial cancellations (user hasn't paid, is high anger risk)
- Instant cancellation
- Clear confirmation: "Your trial has been cancelled. You will not be charged."
