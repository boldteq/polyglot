# Brand Direction — Meridian (sleep & recovery)

## Voice
Calm, clinical-but-warm, evidence-led. Speaks like a knowledgeable pharmacist, never a hype marketer. Short declarative sentences. No exclamation points.

## Visual intent
- Palette: deep midnight navy + warm bone + a single muted amber accent. One accent only.
- Type: a humanist serif for headings (trust, editorial), clean grotesque sans for body.
- Photography: dim, restful, real-skin product-in-hand; no stocky white-bg studio shots.
- Motion: minimal, slow fades; nothing bouncy.

## References (and what to take from each)
1. Ceremonia — the editorial PDP rhythm and ingredient storytelling.
2. Everist — the restrained palette discipline and whitespace.
3. Hims — the clinical-credibility framing without feeling cold.

## Hard constraints
- Must use the existing Meridian wordmark (vector supplied).
- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast on the amber accent (it fails on bone — only on navy).
- No countdown timers, no fake scarcity (honesty gate).
