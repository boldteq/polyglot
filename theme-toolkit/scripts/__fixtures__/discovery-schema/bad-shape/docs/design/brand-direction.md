# Brand Direction — Restful (magnesium sleep)

**Voice:** calm, clinical-but-warm, evidence-led. We sell better sleep, not hype.

**ICP:** stressed 30–45 professionals who wake at 3am and have tried melatonin without luck.

**References:** Ritual (transparency), Headspace (calm restraint), Magic Spoon (review density) — take ingredient honesty, generous whitespace, proof modules.

**Palette/type intent:** deep indigo + warm sand, one serif display, humanist sans body.

**Hard constraints:** no fake urgency, no invented clinical stats, citations required for efficacy claims.
