# Brand Direction — Restful (magnesium sleep)

**Voice:** calm, clinical-but-warm, evidence-led. We sell better sleep, not hype.

**ICP:** stressed 30–45 professionals who wake at 3am and have tried melatonin without luck.

**References:** Ritual (transparency + supplement-facts panel), Headspace (calm palette + generous whitespace), Magic Spoon (playful proof modules) — take Ritual's ingredient honesty, Headspace's restraint, Magic Spoon's review density.

**Palette/type intent:** deep indigo + warm sand, one serif display for headlines, humanist sans for body.

**Hard constraints:** no fake urgency, no invented clinical stats, third-party study citations required for any efficacy claim.
